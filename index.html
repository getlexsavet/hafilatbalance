What Is a Hafilat Card?

The Hafilat Card is a smart travel card used for buses and public transport across Abu Dhabi and surrounding regions. Managed by the Integrated Transport Centre (ITC), the card removes the need for cash and allows passengers to pay fares digitally.

  • Travel on city, regional, intercity, and airport buses
  • Recharge online or offline
  • Track balance and usage
  • Use travel passes for cost savings

Why Hafilat Card Recharge Is Important

Keeping your card topped up ensures uninterrupted travel. A low balance may prevent entry at bus gates or cause delays during boarding. Regular Hafilat recharge helps you:

  • Avoid last-minute issues
  • Manage transport expenses
  • Save time during peak hours

In 2026, the ITC offers multiple secure recharge options, making the process flexible for every user.

How to Check Hafilat Balance in 2026

A Hafilat balance check allows you to see how much credit remains before starting your journey. You can check your balance using several official methods.

Online Balance Check (Official Website)

You can check your Hafilat balance through the official Darb portal by entering your 16-digit card serial number.

Best for: Home or office users

Darbi Mobile App

The Darbi App is the official mobile application for Abu Dhabi public transport services. After logging in, you can view your card balance instantly.

Best for: Smartphone users

Ticket Vending Machines (TVMs)

Available at bus stations and terminals, TVMs show your balance when you tap your card.

Best for: Immediate balance confirmation

SMS Balance Check

You can check balance without internet by sending:

BAL <card-number> to 3009

Best for: Offline users

Hafilat Card Recharge Online Methods

Hafilat Recharge via Official Website

This is one of the most secure and widely used methods for Hafilat recharge online.

  • Visit the official recharge portal
  • Click on “Recharge Card”
  • Enter your 16-digit card number
  • View your current balance
  • Select recharge amount (AED 10–100)
  • Proceed to secure payment
  • Receive SMS confirmation

Payment options include Visa, Mastercard, and UAE-issued debit cards.

Processing time: 24–48 hours

Hafilat Recharge Through Darbi App

  • Download the Darbi App
  • Log in or register
  • Select Public Transport
  • Choose “Recharge Hafilat”
  • Enter card details
  • Complete payment

Processing time: 24–48 hours

Hafilat Recharge Using Payit Mobile Wallet

  • Download Payit App
  • Go to UAE Bills & Recharge
  • Select Hafilat
  • Enter card serial number
  • Choose recharge amount
  • Pay via wallet or linked card

Offline Hafilat Recharge Methods

Ticket Vending Machines (TVMs)

TVMs provide instant balance reflection, making them ideal for urgent recharges.

Payment options: Cash and cards

Banknote Reloader (BR) Machines

These machines accept cash only and provide immediate balance updates.

  • No internet required
  • 24/7 availability
  • Instant processing
  • Exact cash required
  • No card issuance or change

Customer Happiness Centers

If you need staff assistance or instant support, Customer Happiness Centers are the best option.

  • Immediate recharge
  • Card replacement
  • Balance verification

Payment: Cash or card

Balance reflection: Immediate

Authorized Retail Outlets

You can recharge your Hafilat card while shopping at authorized partners.

  • Lulu Hypermarket
  • ADNOC stations
  • Abu Dhabi Co-op
  • Lulu Exchange

Processing time: Up to 24 hours

MBME Recharge Machines

MBME machines are found in commercial buildings and are useful for office workers.

Balance update: Within 2 hours

Types of Hafilat Passes in 2026

  • T-Purse (Pay As You Go): Flexible usage, ideal for occasional travelers
  • 7-Day Pass: Unlimited city rides, suitable for short-term stays
  • 30-Day Pass: Best for daily commuters, cost-effective monthly travel
  • Student Annual Pass: Discounted yearly travel, requires student verification
  • Senior Citizen Annual Pass: Free travel for eligible residents, renewable yearly
  • People of Determination Pass: Free travel with companion access

Tips for Smooth Hafilat Card Usage

  • Check balance before travel
  • Recharge during off-peak hours
  • Enable low-balance alerts
  • Keep receipts for reference
  • Use official apps only
  • Protect your card number

Online recharges typically reflect within 24 to 48 hours, while offline methods like TVMs and BR machines are instant.
